Testnet demo · Updated 4 November 2026

NearPortal is currently a testnet demonstration. Use test assets only. Test tokens and displayed demo rewards are not real money.

Using NearPortal

Use a GitHub account you are authorized to operate. You are responsible for the repositories, code, bounty descriptions and wallet addresses you provide. Do not submit malicious code, impersonate another contributor or attempt to bypass access controls or payment conditions.

Bounties and contributions

Review the bounty’s reward, payment network, acceptance criteria, assignment rules and deadline before funding or claiming it. The agreement shown for that bounty governs its workflow. A claim does not guarantee acceptance or payment.

Only work you are authorized to share should be submitted. Repository access and the rights to contributed code remain subject to the repository’s permissions, license and any agreement between the participants.

Funding and release

Send only the supported test token on the network displayed in the deposit instructions. Release follows the conditions recorded for the bounty, including the applicable verification and client merge requirements. A pending transfer or merge alone is not proof of a completed payout. Use the recorded transaction receipt to verify completion.

Assignments with deadlines may expire and reopen according to their agreed rules. Review the bounty’s displayed release, dispute and refund terms; do not assume every bounty supports the same options.

Demo availability

The prototype may be interrupted, updated or reset. GitHub, blockchain networks, wallet providers and payment services operate independently and may be delayed or unavailable. Do not rely on this demonstration for production work, real funds or permanent storage.

Questions and updates

These terms cover the current testnet demo. Material changes to the service may require updated terms. For general project questions, contact @Nearportal on X. Never send private keys, passwords or other secrets through social messages.
